Map editor

Play ↗

Blocks: drag out a rect. Props and cannons: click a surface; props are decoration, rockets fly through them, F flips one. Drag things to move them, drag edges to resize. Drag empty space or right-drag to pan. Scroll to pan, pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom. PvE: orange pad = players' base, red pad + cannons = CPU. PvP: symmetric, each team's cannons guard its base; build the left half, then Mirror. Saving reloads any open game tab.
